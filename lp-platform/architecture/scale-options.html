L&P Platform Scale Options

The end results leadership has asked for, what the data looks like today, and three scale options to deliver those results, compared layer by layer.

1 · End results

What leadership expects the platform to deliver

Primary

Stronger proposals

  • Accurate, complete, compliant proposals per RFP
  • Know what was strong, what failed, what to change
  • Staffing and price plans ready to approve
Growth

Find and convert business

  • Which buyers to pursue, when, with what advantage
  • Qualified next-contact list with "why now"
  • Which sources and messages win customers
Protection

Protect revenue and clients

  • Authorization gaps and renewals flagged
  • Contract economics and billing exceptions explained
  • Client issues owned before renewal
Readiness

Strengthen capability

  • Verified past performance ready to reuse
  • Credential and eligibility gaps visible
  • Partner, expansion and training plans
Shared

Reusable intelligence

  • Every bid adds knowledge for the next
  • ChatGPT and Claude work from the same records
  • Less repeated research and re-entry

Source: Reference sheet, Overview (intended business results) and Features (human end result per section).

3 · Three scale options

What each option includes, and which results it delivers

Each option is a stage of the same architecture. A grows into B, and B into C, without a rewrite.

IncludedBasic / partialNot included
A · Organized data hubReplace the spreadsheet
B · Intelligence platformRecommended target
C · Enterprise data & AIForecasting at scale

A · Organized data hub

One database and document store replace the spreadsheet. Intelligence work stays mostly manual.

ScaleL&P history
Build1–2 months
Run cost$50–100/mo

Pros

  • Removes spreadsheet limits fast
  • AI agents query one source, not 87 tabs
  • Lowest cost and upkeep

Cons

  • Extraction and entry stay manual
  • No automated competitor or market data
  • No price modeling or forecasting

B · Intelligence platform

Recommended target

Automated document intake, competitor and market data, and pricing and proposal engines turn records into bid intelligence.

Scale1,000s of docs
Build3–6 months
Run cost$150–500/mo

Pros

  • Delivers the core vision across all five results
  • AI reads documents; people confirm
  • Proposals and price sheets built from data

Cons

  • Larger build, more moving parts
  • Staff time to verify extracted facts
  • No forecasting models yet

C · Enterprise data & AI

A warehouse and trained models add win-probability, price-to-win and lead scoring across bids, finance and workforce data.

ScaleMillions of rows
Build6–12+ months
Run cost$400–1,500/mo

Pros

  • Win-probability and price-to-win forecasts
  • Profitability by contract, site, period
  • Scored leads from continuous market data

Cons

  • Models need enough past outcomes
  • Highest cost and complexity
  • Heavy load for one builder

Scale, build time and monthly running cost are rough planning estimates. Costs cover hosting, database, Google Cloud processing and modest AI use, not staff time.

4 · Recommended path

Build A first, grow into B, add C when the data supports it

STAGE A · ~1–2 MONTHSOne source of truth

Database, document store, staff web app, ChatGPT and Claude connected.

Proof: the Data Index sheet migrated; agents answer from one source.

→
STAGE B · ~3–6 MONTHSBid intelligence

Document pipeline, competitor and market feeds, pricing engine, proposal output, QuickBooks and Belfry data.

Proof: one real bid end to end; competitor prices searchable.

→
STAGE C · ~6–12+ MONTHSForecasting

Warehouse history, win-probability and price-to-win models, lead scoring.

Proof: price ranges backed by tested models.
