How the platform is set up

One internal system for finding work, deciding whether to bid, pricing it, writing the proposal, and then running the contract. Google Cloud is the platform. The current application is a draft of the behavior, not the decision about where this runs.

What owners are looking at

One platform, three results

Primary

Stronger proposals

Accurate, complete, buyer-specific responses, built from company proof and from what won or lost before.

Growth

The right pursuits

Find opportunities, understand the buyer, and qualify the work before L&P spends a proposal on it.

Protection

Delivery and renewal

Know the commitment, the actual economics, and the renewal or recompete date before it becomes a surprise.

People and ChatGPT or Claude do the work. The platform keeps the files, the decisions, and the place to resume. Buyers and competitors are records. They do not get logins. Finished bids leave as files and are submitted on the buyer’s own portal, email, or paper.

How a bid moves

Same capabilities, one path

These six jobs are how the work is organized. They are not six separate products.

A · EvidenceCollect the solicitation, company proof, and buyer history. Keep the original file.
B · AnalysisCompare requirements, prices, and past results. Say what is known and what is not.
C · DecisionChoose bid or no-bid, staffing, and price. A person records the decision.
D · DocumentsWrite the proposal, forms, and price schedule in the buyer’s format.
E · RecordSave the exact submission, the outcome, and the contract that follows.
F · CoordinationAssign the work, let an authorized agent continue it, and alert what is due.

Learning loop: the score, the debrief, and the contract’s actual cost come back into evidence. The next proposal starts from that, not from a blank chat.

Cloud Scheduler runs the daily checks: expirations, obligations due, and new public notices. Those checks notify. They do not price, approve, or submit. Cloud Workflows pauses document intake until a person verifies the facts, then continues.

6UseSearch, reports, the proposal, and the agent all read that record.
Prices stay separate

Five figures, never one blended rate

A bid total, a ceiling, and an hourly rate are not the same kind of number. These five stay in their own columns.

Buyer requestedWhat the solicitation asked for.
L&P costWhat it costs to deliver. Not a bill rate.
L&P proposedWhat L&P offered. Final only after a person approves it.
Buyer awardedWhat the award document says.
Current contractWhat applies now, after amendments.

The price L&P submits is a human decision. A model can prepare the comparison. It cannot approve the price.

Also in this platform

The rest of the business uses the same records

Pipeline and outreach

Leads, signals, and follow-up live here as L&P work on buyer records. This does not become a customer portal, and it does not replace the CRM as the system of contact.

Client history

Reviews, issues, and corrective actions are records on the contract. They feed the next bid and the renewal. The client does not log in to see them.

Company readiness

Past performance, licenses, teaming, and training readiness stay current so a proposal can cite them without inventing a credential.
